WORKER SAFETY

WSH control measures

Apply the hierarchy of controls through the task-specific risk assessment and safe work procedure. Have the project RA team verify site conditions.

  1. EliminationWhere practicable, postpone or remove work in unsafe weather or flooded work areas.
  2. SubstitutionUse a lower-exposure work method or off-site prefabrication where suitable.
  3. Engineering controlsMaintain drainage and dewatering, stable access, barriers, and protected electrical equipment; inspect temporary works and excavations.
  4. Administrative controlsMonitor weather, brief workers, set stop-work and restart checks, and inspect the work area before resuming.
  5. PPESelect and inspect task-appropriate PPE as a supplementary control, not a replacement for higher-level controls.
